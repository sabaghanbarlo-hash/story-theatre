Narrator, characters and scenes

With plain text, one character reads the story. If you add characters, an off-screen Narrator reads any line that has no name, and each character speaks only their own lines:

# Characters (voice is optional)
@character Kaito | hair=#2a2030, shirt=#2a9d8f, style=short, voice=am_michael
@character Mia | hair=#6b3a2a, shirt=#e76f51, style=long, voice=af_bella

# New scene and background (words like night, forest, sea, village, dawn change the setting)
[bg: moonlit forest]

Once upon a time, a boy looked up at the moon.
Narrator: Every night he wondered how it would feel to touch it.
Mia: “You can’t catch the moon!”

Voices: af_heart, af_bella, af_nicole, af_sarah, bf_emma, am_michael, am_fenrir, am_puck, bm_george, bm_fable.

Everything runs on your own device and costs nothing. Voicing takes a while on phones; a few minutes of story can take a few minutes to prepare.
